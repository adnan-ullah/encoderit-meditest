# Privacy Policy – Healthcare Homelab

**Last updated:** March 2, 2026  

Healthcare Homelab (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile app (“App”). By using the App, you agree to this policy.  

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Personal info:** Name, phone, address, doctor/referrer info.  
- **Health info:** Test requests, reports, results.  
- **Device & usage info:** Device type, OS, IP address, app usage.  
- **Location:** If you allow, to provide test collection services.  
- **Identifiers:** Firebase IDs or similar for analytics and notifications.  

## 2. How We Use Your Info
- Manage your test requests and prescriptions.  
- Store and show your test history.  
- Communicate updates, reminders, and support.  
- Improve app performance and security.  
- Comply with laws.  

> We **do not sell your information** to third parties.  

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use services like **Google Firebase** and **Google Play Services** for:  
- Secure storage  
- Authentication  
- Notifications  
- Analytics and crash reporting  

These third parties follow their own privacy rules.  

## 4. Permissions
We may ask for:  
- **Location** – to provide services to your address  
- **Camera & Storage** – to upload prescriptions and reports  
- **Phone** – to call support if needed  
- **Notifications** – for updates and reminders  

You can revoke permissions, but some features may not work.  

## 5. Data Retention
We keep your data only as long as needed for services, legal obligations, or dispute resolution. Old data will be deleted or anonymized.  

## 6. Data Security
We use technical and organizational measures to protect your info, but cannot guarantee 100% security.  

## 7. Children’s Privacy
Our App is **not for children under 13**. If you believe a child shared personal info, contact us to delete it.  

## 8. Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may be able to:  
- Access, update, or correct your data  
- Request deletion  
- Withdraw consent  

Contact us to exercise these rights.  

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use of the App means you accept changes.
